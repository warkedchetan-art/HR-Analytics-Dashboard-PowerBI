# HR Analytics Dashboard | Power BI

## Project Overview

The HR Analytics Dashboard is an interactive Power BI project designed to analyze employee attrition and understand workforce trends.

This dashboard helps HR departments monitor employee attrition, analyze employee demographics, explore job roles, and identify areas where employee retention can be improved.

## Objectives

- Analyze employee attrition.
- Monitor the overall attrition rate.
- Understand attrition across different age groups and genders.
- Analyze attrition by job role and education.
- Explore the relationship between employee attrition and total working years.
- Provide meaningful insights to support HR decision-making.

## Tools & Technologies

- Microsoft Power BI
- Excel
- Data Visualization
- Data Analysis

## Dashboard KPIs

- Total Employees
- Attrition Employees
- Average Salary
- Attrition Rate

## Dashboard Analysis

### 1. Attrition by Age Category and Gender
Analyzed employee attrition across different age categories and gender groups to identify workforce trends.

### 2. Attrition by Job Role
Compared employee attrition across different job roles to identify roles with higher employee turnover.

### 3. Attrition by Education
Analyzed employee attrition based on educational backgrounds.

### 4. Attrition by Total Working Years
Explored employee attrition across different experience levels.

### 5. Job Role Analysis
Compared job roles and job levels to understand employee distribution and attrition patterns.

### 6. Interactive Education Field Filter
Added an interactive slicer to filter the dashboard by education field and explore different employee segments.

## Key Insights

- The 25–35 age category shows comparatively high employee attrition.
- Attrition varies across different job roles.
- Laboratory Technicians and Sales-related roles show notable attrition.
- Employee attrition can be analyzed across different education backgrounds.
- Employee experience is an important factor to explore when understanding attrition trends.

## Business Impact

This dashboard helps HR teams:

- Identify employee attrition trends.
- Understand employee turnover across different job roles.
- Explore workforce patterns based on age, gender, education, and experience.
- Support data-driven HR decisions.
- Develop employee retention strategies.
